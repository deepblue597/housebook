Your home’s manual, on a floor plan.

Click the boiler and see its manual, warranty, the fix for error E15 and when it was last serviced. Housebook keeps everything about your house and its appliances in one place you own: plain files on your own server.

Install in 3 steps View on GitHub

What it does

  • A plan of your home

    Draw the rooms, doors, windows and open-plan spaces of each floor and see them in 2D or 3D, with floor finishes and wall paint or wallpaper.

  • Every appliance in one place

    Manual, warranty, quick fixes for common errors, maintenance log, photos and receipts, including receipts kept in Paperless-ngx.

  • What needs attention

    Overdue checks, maintenance due soon and warranties about to end, also as a calendar feed for Google, Apple, Outlook or Thunderbird.

  • Ready for emergencies

    A printable sheet of where the water valve, fuse box and gas valve are and what to do, QR stickers for each appliance, and readable offline.

  • Your files, not a database

    Each item is a Markdown note and the layout is one house.json, in a folder you can back up, sync or open in Obsidian.

  • Household-sized

    One Docker container for amd64 and arm64 (Raspberry Pi 4/5). No login on a home network, or sign in with Keycloak, Authentik or Authelia.

Plain files you can read without it

Every appliance is a Markdown note with a few fields at the top. Edit it in the app, in Obsidian or in any text editor; Housebook notices the change at once. If you ever stop using Housebook, your notes, manuals and receipts stay where they are. 

House/Kitchen/Dishwasher.md
---
type: dishwasher
brand: Bosch
model: SMS4HVI33E
purchased: 2024-05-10
warranty_until: 2027-05-10
attachments: ["[[Dishwasher/manual.pdf]]"]
tags: [housebook]
---

## Quick help

- **E15**: water in the base. Unplug,
  tilt it back, dry it, check the hoses

## Maintenance log

- 2026-03-02 Cleaned the filter

Install in 3 steps

You need Docker with Compose on a machine that stays on: a home server, a NAS or a Raspberry Pi 4/5.

  1. Get the install file

    mkdir housebook && cd housebook
curl -fsSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/deepblue597/housebook/main/deploy/compose.yaml

  2. Make a folder for your house, and the settings

    mkdir data
printf 'HB_UID=%s\nHB_GID=%s\nTZ=Europe/Athens\n' "$(id -u)" "$(id -g)" > .env

    Change Europe/Athens to your time zone. Your house is stored as plain files in data/: back it up.

  3. Start it

    docker compose up -d

    Open http://127.0.0.1:8000 and draw your first room.

Next

  • Phones: set HB_BIND in .env to this machine's address on your home network or tailnet. There is no login in this setup, so never make it reachable from the internet; add a login when you need one.
  • Backups: back up the data folder, or download a zip from the app's ⋯ menu.
  • Upgrading: docker compose pull && docker compose up -d, after a backup. What changed: the changelog.
  • Everything else: settings, reverse proxies, offline use and Paperless-ngx.
